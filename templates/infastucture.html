🏫 School Infrastructure

🏢 School Buildings

Our school building is designed with modern architecture that reflects stability, safety, and creativity. Equipped with spacious classrooms, it provides a comfortable learning atmosphere.

The well-ventilated rooms, clean corridors, and proper natural lighting make the environment ideal for academic excellence and student engagement.

Ensuring safety, the building includes fire-exit points, CCTV monitoring, and earthquake-resistant construction standards.

💻 Computer Labs (2 Labs)

We proudly host two fully-equipped computer labs containing modern desktops, high-speed internet, and digital learning tools for students.

The labs are regularly upgraded to make students familiar with the latest software technology and ICT education standards.

Each lab is supervised by trained instructors ensuring a safe and supportive learning environment for all students.

🧱 Civil Engineering Lab

Our Civil Engineering lab provides hands-on technical education with high-quality tools and testing equipment for practical learning.

Students learn real-world construction skills such as surveying, material testing, and structural analysis.

This lab helps students build confidence and prepares them for technical careers in engineering and architecture.

✏️ Drawing Lab

The drawing lab is a creative hub where students practice engineering drawings, sketches, and drafting techniques.

Equipped with drawing boards, model samples, and measurement tools, it supports accurate and professional learning.

This environment allows students to develop precision, imagination, and technical visualization skills.

📚 Library

Our library is a peaceful and resource-rich space filled with academic books, references, journals, and digital learning materials.

Designed with comfortable seating and bright interiors, it offers a perfect environment for reading and research.

The library promotes self-study habits and helps students explore knowledge beyond textbooks.

🔬 Physics Lab

Our physics lab is equipped with modern scientific instruments for experiments in motion, light, electricity, and energy.

Students perform practical activities that enhance their understanding of scientific concepts and boost analytical skills.

Safety measures and supervision ensure an enjoyable and secure learning experience for all students.

📘 Digital Library

The digital library provides e-books, online courses, and computer-based reference materials for advanced learning.

Students can access educational videos, online notes, and interactive learning modules easily.

It supports modern education through technology and enhances student engagement.
