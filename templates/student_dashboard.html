Welcome to Shree Resunga Secondary School

Shree Resunga Secondary School, established in 2025 BS, is located in Resunga Municipality-2, Udhindhunga, Tamghas, Gulmi. We are committed to providing quality education that nurtures the intellectual, social, and moral development of our students.

  • Academic excellence with modern teaching methods
  • Holistic development through sports, arts, and extracurricular activities
  • Creating responsible and socially-conscious citizens
  • Safe and inspiring learning environment

Our dedicated teachers, supportive staff, and welcoming community ensure a safe and inspiring learning environment for all students. Join us in shaping a brighter future!
