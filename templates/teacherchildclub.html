Child Club Programmes

What is Child Club?

The Child Club aims to empower students to lead, create, and inspire positive changes within the school and community. Through different activities, children learn leadership, confidence, communication, and teamwork skills.

Our Key Objectives

We encourage students to express their creativity, raise awareness on social issues, promote kindness, and develop a sense of responsibility. The club supports the growth of active, responsible young leaders.

Activities & Events

Child Club organizes cultural events, talent shows, school cleanliness campaigns, awareness rallies, celebration programmes, environmental care activities, creative competitions, and community support tasks.

Student Leadership Roles

Students participate as presidents, vice-presidents, secretaries, event managers, media coordinators, and discipline members. These roles help them learn discipline, management, and teamwork.
