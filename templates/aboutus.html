About Our Institution

Legacy of Excellence in Resunga

Founded with a vision to bring quality education to the foothills of Gulmi, Shree Resunga Secondary School has stood as a beacon of academic excellence for decades. We believe that education is more than just textbooks; it is about nurturing curiosity and building character. Our institution provides a safe, inclusive, and stimulating environment where students from diverse backgrounds are encouraged to explore their potential and dream big.

While we take pride in our deep-rooted traditions, we are firmly focused on the future. By integrating modern digital classrooms, well-equipped science laboratories, and a robust physical education program, we ensure our students are ready for the challenges of the 21st century. At Resunga, we don't just teach students to pass exams; we inspire them to become innovative leaders, responsible citizens, and compassionate thinkers that Nepal needs for tomorrow.
