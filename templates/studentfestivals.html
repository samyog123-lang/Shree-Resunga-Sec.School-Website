1.

Dashain

Dashain is Nepal’s biggest festival representing victory, blessings, reunion and joy. Schools remain closed for holidays, allowing students to celebrate with family.

2.

Tihar

Tihar, the festival of lights, is celebrated with diyos, rangoli, Deusi-Bhailo, and worshipping of animals and siblings. Schools remain closed during this joyful celebration.

3.

Chhath

Chhath is a festival dedicated to the Sun God and Chhathi Maiya, marked by fasting, holy bathing, and offering prayers. Schools give holidays during this sacred occasion.
