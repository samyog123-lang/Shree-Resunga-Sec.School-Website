Our Glorious History

Founded in 2025 BS

Shree Resunga Secondary School was established as a vision to provide quality education to the local community. From humble beginnings, it has grown to become a beacon of learning and excellence.

First Graduating Class

The first batch of students graduated with flying colors, laying the foundation for academic rigor and excellence that continues to this day.

ISO Certification

After decades of consistent performance, the school achieved ISO certification, cementing its status as a trusted institution for high-quality education in the region.

Modern Facilities

The school now boasts modern classrooms, laboratories, and libraries, ensuring students have access to the best tools for learning and personal growth.

Our Old School Building

This historic building stands as a symbol of dedication, knowledge, and community. It carries countless memories of students and teachers who helped shape the spirit of Shree Resunga Secondary School.
