Welcome

A Place to Learn, Grow and Succeed

Shree Resunga Secondary School is dedicated to creating an educational environment where students can learn with confidence, explore their interests and develop the knowledge and skills needed for their future.

Education at our school goes beyond textbooks. We encourage students to participate in academic activities, sports, cultural programmes, creative works, community activities and practical learning.

  • Quality and inclusive education
  • Experienced and dedicated teachers
  • Practical and modern learning
  • Student-centered environment
  • Academic and extracurricular development
