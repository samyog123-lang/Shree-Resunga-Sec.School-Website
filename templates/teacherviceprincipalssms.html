Vice Principal's Message

Shree Resunga Secondary School, established in 2025 BS, is a public and ISO-certified institution dedicated to providing quality education. For decades, we have been a center of academic excellence, fostering strong values, innovation, and holistic development. Our goal is to nurture each student’s intellectual, social, and moral growth in a safe and inspiring learning environment. With experienced teachers and modern facilities, we ensure that every child receives the guidance and support needed to reach their full potential.

As Vice Principal, I am committed to working alongside our dedicated staff and the school community to promote creativity, critical thinking, and a love for learning. Beyond academics, we emphasize sports, arts, and extracurricular activities to develop well-rounded individuals. Together, we aim to prepare our students to become responsible, confident, and compassionate citizens ready to thrive in an ever-changing world.

With qualified teachers, modern learning resources, and a supportive environment, we ensure every student discovers their highest potential.
