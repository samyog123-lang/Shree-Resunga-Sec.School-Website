Scouting Programmes

Introduction to Scouting

Our scouting programme helps students develop leadership, discipline, teamwork and real-life survival skills. It encourages students to be active, responsible citizens who contribute positively to society. The programme includes regular training, group activities, community service and skill-based learning.

Outdoor Camps & Adventure

Students participate in trekking, camping, rope climbing, first-aid drills, fire safety training and nature exploration. These activities promote confidence, problem-solving and mental toughness while making students love the outdoors.

Community Service Activities

Scouts of our school regularly organize cleanliness campaigns, awareness rallies, environmental protection programmes and social support events. These activities help students understand responsibility toward society.

Awards & Achievements

Many of our scouts have earned district and regional level awards for excellence in discipline, community service, and leadership tasks. The school is recognized for maintaining an active and dedicated scouting team every year.
