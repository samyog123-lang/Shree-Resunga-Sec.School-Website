Red Cross Programmes

About the Red Cross Programme

The Red Cross Circle of our school works to develop humanitarian values among students. It encourages volunteerism, empathy, first-aid knowledge, and community service to help those in need during emergencies.

Our Mission

To cultivate discipline, safety awareness, social responsibility, and emergency response skills in students. The Red Cross builds a generation of caring, alert, and helpful youths.

Major Activities

First-aid Training, Blood Donation Awareness, Health Camps, Cleanliness Programmes, Disaster Preparedness Drills, Environmental Care Drives, and Social Welfare Events.

Student Responsibilities

Members take part as health leaders, first-aid helpers, discipline managers, awareness volunteers, and community-support coordinators.
